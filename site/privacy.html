Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version: your budget stays on your phone. Vinnie has no accounts, never asks for your bank login, and doesn't collect your data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter in Vinnie (purchases, amounts, notes, categories, budgets and your settings) is saved in a database on your own device. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. We can't see it, and we have no copy of it.

Because it lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes your data with it.

What we collect

Nothing. Vinnie doesn't need an account, doesn't connect to your bank, and doesn't use advertising or tracking. We don't sell or share personal data, because we don't have any.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Vinnie schedules reminders on your device. They are created and delivered locally; no notification data passes through our servers.

Sharing

Some screens let you share an image, for example a moment with Vinnie. Nothing is shared unless you tap share, and then it goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

Vinnie is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

This website

getvinnie.app keeps a count of likes. Pressing the like button adds one to the total; we don't store who pressed it. The demo on this site saves what you enter in your own browser only.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If Vinnie ever starts collecting any data, we'll say so here first.
