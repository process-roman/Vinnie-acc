Vinnie Personal budget app

The budget app that yells at you.

Your budget has a guy. Vinnie helps you break bad spending habits: he tracks every purchase, and he gets mad when you overspend.

  • Log a purchase in seconds
  • Set a monthly budget, with a limit for each category
  • See where your money went, month by month
  • Overspend and he punches through your screen, then calls you
  • Stick to your budget and he's proud of you. He won't admit it
